Play without an account. Your progress, records and settings are saved on your device. The current game has no advertising, analytics SDKs or in-app purchases.

Who we are

TapeeTepee openstudio operates TAPtoTEN. This policy covers the TAPtoTEN Android game, its web version and support enquiries. Privacy and support contact: wsndydtml@gmail.com.

Game data on your device

We save your tutorial progress, best scores and times, daily play records, and music, sound and vibration preferences. Android uses the app's private preferences storage; the web version uses your browser's local storage. These records are used to resume play and show your records. The game does not send them to a developer-operated server or share them with advertising or analytics services.

The game does not request your name, date of birth, location, contacts, camera or microphone. There are no accounts, chat, public profiles or player uploads.

Platform services and the website

Android may back up or restore app data according to your device and Google account settings. We do not operate or have access to that backup service, and restoration is not guaranteed. Google Play and your operating system handle store, security and diagnostic information under their own policies and settings.

When you visit the web version or this policy online, our website host, Vercel, receives information needed to serve the page, such as your IP address, request details and browser information, and may retain technical logs for delivery and security. This is different from playing the bundled Android game. We do not add advertising cookies or analytics tracking to the game or this page.

If you contact us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to answer your request and resolve support or privacy issues. Email is handled through our email provider, Google Gmail. Please send only information needed for your enquiry and do not send passwords or sensitive personal details.

We do not sell personal information or use support messages for advertising. Hosting and email providers process information as needed to provide their services; information may also be disclosed if legally required.

Children and families

TAPtoTEN welcomes children and adults. Children can play without providing personal information. We do not use behavioural advertising, advertising identifiers or tracking SDKs in the game. Parents or guardians should contact us on a child's behalf. If we learn that a child has sent personal information that we do not need, we will delete it unless retention is legally required.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game records remain until you clear the app's storage, clear this site's browser data, or remove the app; platform backups may exist separately. A normal update is designed to preserve records, but uninstalling, clearing data or changing phones may lose them. Manage platform backups through your device or Google account settings.

We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the enquiry or meet legal obligations, then delete it. To request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us, contact wsndydtml@gmail.com. We cannot access or remotely erase records stored only on your device. There is no game account to delete.

Security and changes

The Android game stores its records in app-private storage. Protect your device and email account; no storage or transmission method is completely secure. We will update this policy and the date above when the game's data practices change.
